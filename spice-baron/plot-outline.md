# Spice Baron
A near future southern gothic science fiction novel set in the aftermath of a global apocalypse. Gardner lives on a plantation run by a cruel sadist who calls himself the Baron. This document gives a rough outline of the plot.

## Act 1
- Gardner forced to watch execution of the chef. Execution is a sort of take on Blood Eagle. horrific.
- Gardner escapes to refuge of the massive spice growing greenhouses. Baron follows and taunts him. Speech about power an ownership. Having is the most important thing. informs of new chef.
- New Chef arrives during cinnamon peeling. Baron excited that Chef is prestigious chef from Yoruba

## Act 2
- Emissary arrives from Yoruba. They want to negotiate spice trading terms.
- Gardner and the Chef become involved romantically.
- Become closer and Chef teaches him to cook a little.
- Chef wants to go on a collection trip to Yoruba. There are things there that the Baron does not have. Imagine what they could do with the right materials, while they languish in the botanical gardens there.

## Act 3
- Gardener's eyes opened to Yoruba. The parks and plants and conservatories. The beautiful buildings and people and places.
- Finds out about plot to take over the spice plantation. 
- Gardner gives testimony. Decision made.
- Return to plantation. Gardner dreams of future.

## Act 4
- Gardner woken to shouts and guards. Dragged out to courtyard.
- Chef in the stockade. Baron executes him brutally. Gardner forces to watch again.
- Gardner to prepare a meal, maybe he'll be gardner and chef. Someone the Baron knows he can trust.
- Gardner gathering ingredients and remembering, preparing, cooking.
- Starts the dinner with wine. Pours it for Baron. The 'Girls' (Baron's Harem he let's his guard use freely), pour wine for their guards. Gardner watches expectantly and Baron notices, stops, "why don't you take the first sip. After back and forth, Gardner takes the first sip. Hands the cup back. Baron watches for a little and talks a little. satisfied it isn't poison he drinks the rest as do the guards.
- Baron begins talking, gets more feverish, Gardner starting to hallucinate. Baron notices something off and asks what he put in the drink. Gardner explains it is a hallucinogen that helps people see the true beauty of the world. Gardner gives speech.
- Baron calls for guards, but the Girls step forward and slit the throats of the stupefied guards.
- Baron carried out by guards to the courtyard.
- Gardner "blooms him". this passage is full of imagery describing the peeling of the baron in botanical terms. Gardner places a mirror in front of the Baron so he has to watch his own death. 

## Epilogue
(narration note - Switch perspective)
- Emissary/invasion party from Yoruba arrives. finds the spent corpse of the Baron strung up like a sculpture.
- Go into the hall and find Gardner draped across the chair. He gives speech that he is the caretaker of the plantation. He will give them trade, but they will respect the beauty of this place. The girls are now arranged as his own guards, dressed in a mix of armor and their tattered gowns.
