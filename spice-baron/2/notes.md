## 1.3.2 Emissary from Brimstone
bring strong chattle-boys for the army. The hands are an ominous, silent force. The ears and curiously stoic, almost not real people, seeming to be everywhere, but saying almost nothing. The king is a mad philosopher (this is a good link to The Ranger story, where the old ranger meets the man who will become king, who is one of the men that Moses and his partner experimented on). The old mouth is a deranged prophet spouting nonsense. The young prophet is a scared child.

## 1.3.4 Emissaries from the clans
The clans have warriors, but don't much care for the Brimstone rigidity. They live out in the sticks, and war sometimes between each other. There are family feuds, but those can be set aside for the sake of staving off empire.
