# Act 4 - Movement 1

# 4.1.1 - return
Gardner and Taiwo return from Yoruba bringing back incredible new plants and flavors and other exotics. The Baron gives no hints of suspicion

# 4.1.2 - found out
Taiwo wakes Gardner up, they've been found out, they have to escape. They make it as far as the grounds, but they are caught by the Baron.

# 4.1.3 - taiwo's reckoning
Gardner is brought out and placed in the witness's seat for Taiwo's reckoning. He would not want to look away. Taiwo speaks to him through the pain. Rattles off beautiful things until he can no longer speak and the life goes out of him.

# 4.1.4 - you will always belong to me (Baron to Gardner)
Gardner languishes in a cell and is visited by the Baron. He stands up for himself and talks back to the Baron. The Baron sees it as sport. Gardner was broken the day he met him, now he will have the pleasure of breaking him properly.
