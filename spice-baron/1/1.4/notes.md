## 1.4.3 Emissaries from the other plantations
The loose network of plantations that orbit Yoruba all the way up to the coast do not like the increasing éntitlement of the young city state and they fear it has empire in mind. If they have to join, they would rather do so with someone like them than the loud and colorful citizens of the Jewel of the World. This is perhaps a ball? Maybe a debut. We want to show some heterogeneity, but they pretend to be in high society. They have smaller plantations like the barons. It was something of a fad started by the first degall. His other rich friends sought to recreate a sort of southern royalty, but it had putrefied into pageantry in decaying lace. They all envy the baron, and he loves it. He uses Yoruba as a scape goat and explains that he has been amassing wealth so that they might all prosper one day, independent.
