# Scene 2
We have already established The Baron and Gardner at this point. We have a strong picture of Gardner's life. Scene 2 is where we are going to interrupt the status quo by introducing Taiwo.

## 1.2.1 - cinnamon peeling
i want to somehow work in Michael Ondantjee's poem the cinnamon peeler. It may not be here, but it is a beautiful poem about belonging, possessing, and being marked by love. it has passion, violence and romance that fit well in this story.

The robots dance in circles and cut the bark open with a laser. the smell of roast cinnamon is thick in the air. He tries to give himself to it and be lost in it. Gardner is stacking the collars beside the trees. It's hot and Gardner has his shirt off. He hears the door open, and he cringes. The Baron's the only one who visits here. Baron calls and he turns around. The Baron is accompanied by another man. Baron introduces him to the new chef. Gardner all of a sudden feels naked and puts on his shirt. Taiwo says not to bother on his account. It's so hot, he's tempted to do the same.   

## 1.2.2 - the new chef
Baron says that this chef is the jewel of Yoruba. A culinary genius. The envy of the world. The new chef introduces himself as Taiwo. The new chef has a confidence and exuberance that's unusual on the estate. Taiwo addresses Gardner directly, asking him questions about the plantation, goes to one of the collars and scrapes some of the dust and tastes it (Gardner shocked that the Baron allows this), asking if this or that is grown here. The Baron says everything is grown here. Gardner corrects the Baron and instantly regrets it, but Taiwo is able to diffuse the situation by suggesting they will have to steal some from Yoruba. It needs a real gardner to tend it. Yoruban horticulture is decades behind this place. The Baron feels pride in his possession of Gardner again. Baron says that's why they are there actually. They need to collect ingredients for a feast that's coming up in a week's days time. The baron leaves Gardner to get Taiwo anything he needs. It's a very important dinner, and a chance for Taiwo to show off his talents. 

## 1.2.3 - collecting ingredients for dinner
Gardner asks Taiwo what he needs. Taiwo says he isn't sure yet. He needs to see the whole garden, but he's particular curious about fruits and chile peppers. They wander through the various chambers chatting about the operations. Is it just you? Taiwo asks. Just me, and the tenders, Gardner said. They watch one of the robots shuttle down a track. 'Must get lonely'. Gardner doesn't know how to respond. 'Yes, let's continue'. They travel some more. Finally, they get to a fruit bearing plant (maybe a cactus? maybe a fig?). Taiwo doesn't recognize it, asks if it is edible. Gardner says yes. Taiwo asks what it tastes like. Gardner says he doesn't know, they are not supposed to eat them. Taiwo plucks one and cuts a piece out with his knife and gives a piece to Gardner. Taiwo thinks for a bit, then pulls salt out of his little hip pouch. hands it again. Gardner blown away by the flavor not of salt but like the fruit but more intense. Then sugar. then vinegar. 

We aren't supposed to eat these. Nonsense! Taiwo says. How are we (gardner thrills at the word we) supposed to create the best cuisine the world has ever seen if we can't taste the world! I was born a twin, when twins are born in my culture, they are given the names Taiwo and Kehinde. Kehinde, my sister, is the older and she sent me out into the world to taste it and tell her if it is good. I guess I never stopped. The world is such a delicious place.

Maybe worth adding in here that Gardner's synaesthesia is intense and Taiwo sees it. He asks what's wrong and Gardner tries to describe it. Taiwo knows too, because he is also synaesthetic. He used to struggle as a child, but then he learned that he could learn to bring it all together. It was a strength, a gift, not a weakness or a curse.

## 1.2.4 - tasting the world
The rest of the tour becomes almost indulgent. Gardner becomes so curious about Taiwo. He takes him to all of the plants he ahs been curious about that he has never tasted. It is as if his life had been lived in only a single color and now Taiwo and tilted the light through a prism and scattered it into a million bands of color distinct and bleeding into each other. Each time, they taste with salt, then sweet, then sour.
Gardner crashes into his bed again and even though he closes his eyes, he can still see and hear and taste all those colors.
